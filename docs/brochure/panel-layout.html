Parts key
Q1Panel isolator 2-pole. Switches the whole panel off for maintenance.
F16 A MCB protecting the controller supply.
PS1DIN power supply, 230 V AC to 24 V DC for the controller.
PM1PZEM-004T meter, CT clamp on the house live: voltage, current, energy.
U2/U3AC detection on the NEPA and GEN inputs (before the changeover). Neither live but house powered = inverter.
A1DIN ESP32 controller with 4 relays and inputs. Talks to the hub over Wi-Fi.
K1/K2Contactors. A1's relay only switches the coil; the contactor carries the pump or AC current.
X1Terminals for every incoming and outgoing wire, plus earth.

Electrician only for 230 V. Q1, F1, PS1, PM1, U2, U3, K1/K2 and all mains terminals must be installed by a qualified electrician to local regulations. Size cables and contactors to the actual pump rating. If A1 loses contact with the hub, relays release and the pump stops (fail-safe).
