Cheaper way to start

Monitoring only, no switching: controller, power meters, diesel sensor, inverter reader and hub. About ₦330k – ₦640k, less if an existing computer runs the hub. You see the diesel, power and battery picture first and add switching later.

How it pays back

If A.D.A. stops 20 litres a week being wasted, siphoned or short-delivered, at ₦1,300 per litre that is about ₦113,000 a month. The parts pay back in roughly 4 – 7 months, before counting generator hours saved or equipment protected.
